== extends "/templates.inc/switches/blog.html" == block toml title = "Blogging Tools Are Back Online" status = "done" created = 2026-11-21T18:46:08-05:00 id = "01/ng/4b/a0" flags = [] tags = [] == endblock == block main == filter md I haven't written in a while. Part of that is becaue I haven't had the energy to. Part of __that__ is because the tools I use to blog were in a state of disarray. Two things triggered the situation. First, I started the move from [alanwsmith.com](https://alanwsmith.com) to this site ([al9000.com](https://al9000.com)). An open-ended, ongoing process that I'll chip away at over time1. Second I switched my writing app from from Neovim to Emacs2. Combined, those changes introduced a lot of structural work to redo the way text goes from my computer to the internet. I started the work earlier this year. Then, as they say, life happened. Nothing bad, just stuff that required a lot of time, energy, and attention with a side helping of stress thrown in for good measure. I went for months without doing any of the work necessary to get things updated so I could post. The knock on effect was I wasn't writing or making new things for the site. That was fine for a while. Then, I hit a point where I wanted to make stuff but couldn't becaue the tools were borked. Working on tooling takes a differnet kind of mental energy and effort. I didn't have that in me. The problem is that not doing creative work is bad for my mental health. It's one of the ways I recharge. Since I couldn't make stuff because the tools were offline, I struggled to get the energy necessary to fix the tools. Energy deadlock. Today, I finally broke though. Having just enough energy to work on the tooling. Getting it to the point where I can make basic posts again3. Feels good to be back. -a
