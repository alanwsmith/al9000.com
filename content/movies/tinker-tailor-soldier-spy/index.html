No. (Unless I read the book first. Something I'm not likely to do since I already know the ending) ## Details - Released 2011 - Directed by: Tomas Alfredson - The lead is Gary Oldman. - The rest of the cast is stellar: Benedict Cumberbatch, John Hurt, and Tom Hardy amoung them. ## Notes - Watched it in November or 2026. - Recommended by a buddy as one of their favorite spy films. - Based on a book of the same name. - I watched it kinda late at night. The film is very quiet and slow paced. I might have enjoyed it more if I'd seen it in a less tired state. I kinda doubt that though. Shots tend to linger without dialog (and without me being invested) long enough that I found my mind wandering out of the movie frequently. - I'm guessing I would have enjoyed the film a lot more if I'd read the book. There's just so much space in the movie where "show don't tell" didn't hold me. - The run time is 2 hours 7 minutes. I could have been 30% shorter without losing much. - Oldman's acting is very quiet and reserved. It's great in it's own right, but with the pace of the movie it almost feels like they're fucking with you in terms of how long scenes take to develop. - Overall, I just didn't get invested in the story. ## Links - [The Wikipedia Article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinker_Tailor_Soldier_Spy_(film)) == endfilter == endblock
