    • "They live so long... but the good ones still bond with us for our entire lives."

My only hope is that I am able to last until his final moments. The death of one of his kind is so rare. The ending of a life so long is such a tragedy. He has seen so much. He knows so much. I know he takes comfort in my presence. I only wish that I will be able to give him this comfort until the end."
